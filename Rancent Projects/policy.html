Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15 July 2026

Welcome to Rancet Projects Pty, We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website to inquire about or request electrical services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing an interest in obtaining information about our electrical services or requesting a quote. This includes:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address or location
  • Details regarding your electrical service requirements

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide you with accurate service quotes and estimates.
  • Schedule and manage electrical call-outs, repairs, or installations.
  • Communicate with you regarding service updates, appointments, or inquiries.
  • Comply with applicable local legal and regulatory data protection requirements.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers solely for the purpose of operating our business, such as dispatching technicians or processing billing, under strict confidentiality agreements.
