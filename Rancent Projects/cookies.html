Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 15 July 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior data. This helps us understand how visitors use our site and allows us to improve the browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Our website uses the following types of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for the website to function properly, such as allowing you to securely submit contact and quote request forms without interruption.
  • Analytical Cookies: Used to track website traffic and user interaction, helping us optimize our marketing efforts, understand local service demand, and improve the website layout.

3. Managing Cookies

You can set your browser not to accept cookies, and you can remove cookies at any time from your browser settings. However, please note that disabling cookies may impact the functionality of certain website features, such as our inquiry forms.
